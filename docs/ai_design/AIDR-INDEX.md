## What are AIDRs?
AI Decision Records (AIDRs) govern the AI-specific architecture, including LLM gateways, prompting strategies, RAG pipelines, structured output validation, agentic orchestration frameworks, evaluation methodologies, and safety protocols.

AIDRs are added as AI decisions surface and evolve throughout the implementation of AI components.
